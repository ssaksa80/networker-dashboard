Install your own certificate

  1. Get a certificate and key in PEM format from your CA, issued to the name people type to reach the dashboard.
  2. Start the service with --cert and --key pointing at them, and --no-auto-cert. With the installer, re-run Setup-NWDash.cmd with its certificate options.
  3. Restart the service, then check this page: the issuer should be your CA.
